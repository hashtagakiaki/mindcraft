# Single-tree felling and pillar recovery — 2026-10-07

`skills.fellTree(bot, options)` selects a natural single-trunk tree, follows its same-species logs through diagonal branches, cuts them, collects the drops, and removes/reclaims the temporary pillars it placed. Usage and supported boundaries are in [README](../README.md#fell-one-tree-and-clean-up-its-pillars).

## Observed results

Direct public SDK calls ran under the existing operation owner against a disposable vanilla 1.21.1 CaseServer on `127.0.0.1:25569`. The evaluator copied its existing oak-grove template; the copied world received a flat grass test area. The ordinary tree was a five-log fixture with natural leaves. The taller tree was generated by Minecraft's `minecraft:fancy_oak` configured feature and had eleven levels of logs and branches.

## Corrections supported by the isolated checks

- Mineflayer emits item metadata updates after item entities merge. Reading the current stack at `playerCollect` avoids undercounting merged logs.
- A branch ray can intersect a leaf's face while the leaf's center remains obstructed. The skill recursively resolves eligible obstructions and retains the existing visibility/dig admission checks.
- Turning toward a pillar during a jump can use up its placement window. The skill finishes aiming before jumping and allows a movement packet interval before placing. Placement errors retain observed pillar positions for cleanup.
- Drops can settle on leaves in another branch after the original pillar has been removed. They remain work targets even after every log is gone; another tracked pillar can reach them and release the supporting natural leaves.
- A block-corner movement goal can finish outside item pickup reach. Final pickup uses the existing `GoalFollow(item, 1)` pattern with restricted movements, after waiting for falling drops to settle.

## Verification scope

`tests/tree_felling.test.cjs` uses disposable mocks and the installed Vec3, Minecraft registry and pathfinder goals. It checks normal and leaf-obscured tall branched trees, distant canopy-drop recovery after all logs are gone, fractional-position ground pickup, merged drops, multiple grounded trunks, unloaded boundaries, insufficient materials, ambiguous placement errors, and cancellation after digging or placement. It also checks exported skill documentation, movement restrictions and listener cleanup. The fixture is included in `tests/run-tests.cjs`.

The live checks establish the direct skill's physical behavior on the two surveyed fixtures. They do not establish success on every tree/terrain arrangement, background Agent modes, or model-driven skill selection. Connected multiple trunks and a branch pillar without same-level natural ground deliberately return unfinished results. Cancellation starts no cleanup world mutations; returned pillar coordinates may need later recovery. The existing manual-play source pin and running bots were not updated.
